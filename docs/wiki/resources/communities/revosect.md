## What it is

Revosect is an aim-training and esports-improvement community that runs its own benchmark seasons, training routines, and guides, alongside a Discord server, as described on the [Revosect homepage](https://revosect.com). One-on-one and group coaching is planned but listed as "coming soon" rather than live, per the same page.

## Who it suits

Players who want a tiered benchmark to measure raw aim, similar in spirit to [Voltaic](voltaic.md), plus curated practice routines for specific games. Revosect's [benchmarks](https://revosect.com/benchmarks) are split into three difficulty tiers, Entry, Intermediate, and Advanced, so both newer and experienced players have a track to climb; the top Advanced ranks require a recorded VOD to verify scores.

## What it covers

- **Benchmarks**: the current flagship benchmark on Revosect's site is Season 5, built for [KovaaK's](../trainers/kovaaks.md), covering six subcategories under [Clicking](../../skills/clicking.md) (static, dynamic), [Tracking](../../skills/tracking.md) (precise, reactive), and [Switching](../../skills/switching.md) (speed, evasive), per the [benchmarks page](https://revosect.com/benchmarks). Revosect has also published benchmark seasons and progression sheets for [Aimlabs](../trainers/aimlabs.md), currently at Season 2, listed on its [resources page](https://revosect.com/resources).
- **[Routines](../../training/routines.md)**: foundational Aimlabs routines plus weakness- and smoothness-specific routines, and separate game-specific practice routines and playlists for titles including Valorant, Apex Legends, Overwatch 2, Call of Duty/Warzone, Battlefield, Counter-Strike 2, and Fortnite, per the [resources page](https://revosect.com/resources).
- **Guides**: general aiming terminology and a beginner-oriented guide to Aimlabs benchmarks, also on the resources page.

## Key content

- [Revosect Resources](https://revosect.com/resources): index of Revosect's benchmarks, progression sheets, guides, and game-specific routines.
- [Revosect Benchmarks](https://revosect.com/benchmarks): rules, difficulty tiers, and scoring for the current Season 5 KovaaK's benchmark.

## Our take

Revosect covers similar ground to [Voltaic](voltaic.md): benchmarks and routines across both KovaaK's and Aimlabs, plus a Discord community. Its current benchmark focus leans toward KovaaK's, while its routine library leans toward Aimlabs and game-specific practice for a wide range of shooters beyond the two main trainers. Its own rank names and season numbering are separate from Voltaic's, so scores between the two are not directly comparable. Coaching is not yet available, unlike Voltaic's Amped program.
