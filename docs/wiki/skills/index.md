Aim in a shooter breaks down into three underlying mechanical skills that show up in almost every
scenario and benchmark: clicking, tracking, and switching. [Aimlabs](../resources/trainers/aimlabs.md)
treats these as aim's three foundational categories and recommends a training playlist draw from
all three rather than lean on one, per
[How to Build a Well Balanced Aim Training Playlist](https://aimlabs.com/articles/aimlabs/how-to-build-a-well-balanced-aim-training-playlist/).
Each family below splits into three subcategories; the names used on this wiki follow
[Voltaic](../resources/communities/voltaic.md)'s Season 5 KovaaK's benchmark, per the
[Season 5 announcement](https://blog.voltaic.gg/announcing-the-voltaic-season-5-aiming-benchmarks-beta-for-kovaaks/).

| Skill | Subcategories | Short description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Clicking](clicking.md) | Dynamic, Static, Linear | Acquiring a target and timing a single click or short burst on it. |
| [Tracking](tracking.md) | Precise, Reactive, Control | Keeping the crosshair on a target that's already moving. |
| [Switching](switching.md) | Speed, Evasive, Stability | Moving the crosshair from a cleared target to the next one. |
