Guides are signed, first-person pages: how to attack a specific problem, how to break a hard
scenario into parts you can actually train, how to get unstuck on a scenario that has stalled.

They work differently from the rest of this wiki, and the difference is deliberate. Every other
page is written from public sources with each claim linked back to where it came from. A guide is
written from experience, so it carries its author's name at the top instead of a citation trail.
That is the whole trust model: if you trust the person who wrote it, the guide is worth your time,
and if you do not, it is not. Judge it on the name, and on whether the method works when you try
it.

Guides are opinions, so expect them to disagree with each other eventually, and to disagree with
how you already train. That is fine. Nothing here overrides the sourced pages under
[Fundamentals](../fundamentals/index.md) or [Skills](../skills/index.md); a guide is one person's
route through that material, not a correction to it.

## Writing one

Guides are written by this wiki's maintainer for now. Authorship is not open to contributors yet;
corrections and suggestions are welcome as issues on the
[source repository](https://github.com/Bassel-Bakr/aim). See
[CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/Bassel-Bakr/aim/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for the page template
and the byline rule.
