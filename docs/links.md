Guides, articles, and videos that teach something, grouped by what they help with. The
[communities](wiki/resources/index.md), [trainers](wiki/resources/index.md), and
[tools](wiki/resources/index.md) pages cover who publishes what; this page points at specific
pieces worth your time.

Everything here explains a method, a mechanism, or a mistake. Benchmark announcements, score
sheets, and routines handed over without an explanation of how to play them are left to the
resource pages.

- [Zeonlo's Static Guide 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4cdQpKCCOGM) — Zeonlo, 2026 —
  tension, fluidity, pathing, and playstyles in six minutes, opening with the Bardoz method.
- [This simple aim tech makes flicking 10x easier](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PEkz4YncixM) —
  ARandomDeadDude, 2025 — how to find the undershoot distance that suits you, and how to drill it

  the same thing as underaiming above.
